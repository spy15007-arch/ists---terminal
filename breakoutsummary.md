## 📌 Technical Metric Key
* **Readiness Score (/10):** Multi-factor rating combining close tightness, volume surge, base range, relative strength, and overhead resistance.
* **Close Pos %:** Position of price close within the day's High-Low range (100% = day's high).
* **Vol vs 50d:** Today's volume divided by the 50-day average volume (>1.5x = institutional buying).
* **Base Range %:** Width of prior 50-day consolidation range (smaller = tighter base).
* **RS Edge %:** 3-Month performance outperformance over the NIFTY 50 index.
* **Resistance Clearance %:** Distance to 50-day high (0.0% = making new highs).
