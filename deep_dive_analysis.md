### 2. Why Did the Stock Fall Earlier?

PNB experienced a major multi-year de-rating between 2018 and 2022, driven by severe structural balance sheet impairments and operational missteps:

1.  **The 2018 Fraud Event & Letters of Undertaking (LoUs):** The discovery of an unauthorized $2 billion (~₹14,000+ Cr) fraudulent transaction involving Nirav Modi/Gitanjali Gems exposed severe internal auditing and swift-system integration failures, causing massive provisioning hits and erosion of capital buffers.
2.  **Legacy Non-Performing Asset (NPA) Super-Cycle:** Aggregate Gross NPAs breached ~18.4% in FY18–FY19 due to unhedged exposure to stressed infrastructure, steel, and power sector corporate conglomerates.
3.  **Triple-Bank Merger Integration Friction (2020):** The amalgamation of Oriental Bank of Commerce (OBC) and United Bank of India (UBI) into PNB added significant operational complexity, asset quality harmonization delays, and IT infrastructure migration costs.
4.  **Capital Dilution & PCA Threat:** High credit costs wiped out net profits, forcing regulatory capital injections from the Government of India and substantial equity dilution at distressed valuations.

### 2. Why Did the Stock Fall Earlier?

Poonawalla Fincorp experienced structural and sentiment-driven drawdowns during prior cycles due to four core operational and macro factors:

1. **Legacy Portfolio Cleanup & Magma Fincorp Transition:** Following the acquisition of Magma Fincorp by Rising Sun Holdings (Adar Poonawalla), the business underwent an aggressive, high-friction portfolio legacy write-off. Non-performing legacy commercial vehicle and housing assets were run down or divested, causing short-term optical pain in net interest margins (NIM) and reported profitability.
2. **Regulatory Risk Weights Increase on Retail Unsecured Lending:** RBI’s November 2023 mandate raising risk weights on consumer credit by 25 percentage points created sector-wide headwinds for fast-growing retail NBFCs. Market participants priced in potential capital tier-1 erosion and NIM compression across POONAWALLA's consumer loan book.
3. **Divestment of Magma Housing Finance (Short-term Earnings Vacuum):** The strategic sale of its housing subsidiary (Magma Housing Finance) to TPG resulted in a transient drop in overall consolidation AUM scale, prompting dynamic institutional re-allocation pending redeployment of sale proceeds.
4. **Elevated Cost of Funds during Rate Hike Cycle:** The sharp 250 bps rate hike cycle by the RBI compressed spreads across non-deposit taking NBFCs before Poonawalla secured its AAA credit rating upgrades, which later lowered borrowing yields.
