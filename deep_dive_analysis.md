### 2. Why Did the Stock Fall Earlier?

Following its public listing, ACME Solar experienced price volatility and supply pressure due to several operational and market-wide factors:

1. **Post-IPO Price Discovery & Anchor Lock-In Expiry:** Supply overhang from post-listing liquidity adjustments and short-term tactical exit by non-core pre-IPO investors.
2. **Input Cost Volatility (ALMM & PV Modules):** Uncertainties regarding the enforcement of the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM) and fluctuations in imported solar module prices temporarily elevated capital expenditure expectations.
3. **Execution & Grid Connectivity Lags:** Broad market concerns over India’s transmission infrastructure (ISTS capacity bottlenecks) created temporary fears of delayed commissioning for utility-scale solar projects.
4. **Sector-Wide Midcap/Smallcap De-rating:** A macro correction across Indian clean-tech and capital goods counters compressed high-multiple renewable assets during broader market drawdowns.

**Yes, a structural operational and financial recovery is underway.**

* **De-leveraging via Primary Capital:** Proceeds from the fresh issue have been allocated toward debt paydown, directly reducing interest costs and lowering financial risk.

* **Stabilization of Solar Cell & Module Costs:** Softening raw material prices (polysilicon/wafers) globally paired with domestic sourcing partnerships have restored project-level Internal Rates of Return (IRRs).
* **Technical Rebound:** Price action moving above all critical moving averages with high volume confirms structural institutional re-accumulation.

### 5. Fundamental Analysis

ACME Solar operates as one of India's prominent Independent Power Producers (IPPs) in the renewable energy sector, focusing on utility-scale solar, wind, and hybrid projects.

* **Revenue Visibility:** Long-term PPAs (typically 25 years) with high-credit rating central utilities (SECI, NTPC) provide predictable, annuity-like cash flows.
* **Operational Portfolio:** Rapidly expanding operational capacity from ~1.3 GW towards a total portfolio (operational + under-construction) exceeding 6.5+ GW.
* **Margin Profile:** IPP business model yields high EBITDA margins (~85%–90% at operational asset level), driven by low operating expenses once plants achieve Commercial Operation Date (COD).
* **Capital Efficiency:** Shift toward hybrid and storage-backed PPAs allows ACME Solar to command higher realization tariffs compared to plain-vanilla solar assets.

### 10. Key Risks

1. **Grid Transmission Delays:** Delays by Power Grid Corporation or state agencies in providing grid connectivity could defer COD timelines.
2. **Off-taker Risk:** While exposure is predominantly to central agencies (SECI/NTPC), any exposure to state DISCOMs introduces potential payment cycle delays.
3. **Interest Rate Risk:** Higher-for-longer benchmark interest rates increase project financing costs and compress project equity IRRs.
4. **Foreign Exchange Risk:** Imports of solar modules/inverters remain exposed to USD/INR volatility if unhedged.
