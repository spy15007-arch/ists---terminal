### 2. Why Did the Stock Fall Earlier?

Aarti Industries experienced a multi-quarter de-rating from its peak levels due to a confluence of global cyclical headwinds, operational cost inflation, and margin compression across its key product chains:

```
1. Global Agrochemical & Specialty Chem Inventory De-stocking
   ├── Post-COVID over-ordering led to record high channel inventories globally.
   └── Agrochemical clients halted fresh orders, causing severe demand destruction.

2. Chinese Dumping & Price Deflation
   ├── Post-lockdown Chinese capacity additions flooded international markets.
   └── Benzene and Nitrotoluene derivative realizations collapsed by 25-40%.

3. Raw Material & Utility Cost Spikes
   ├── Volatile crude oil prices inflated key feedstocks (Benzene, Toluene).
   └── Higher energy costs in domestic operations compressed gross spreads.

4. High Operating Leverage & Financial Costs
   ├── Massive capital expenditures (Capex) commissioned right before the downcycle.
   └── Debt/Equity rose to ~83.4%, triggering elevated interest expense during low EBITDA quarters.
```
