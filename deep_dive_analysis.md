### 2. Why Did the Stock Fall Earlier?

Prior cyclical pullbacks and margin compressions in BECTORFOOD were driven by four distinct macroeconomic and operational headwinds:

1. **Severe Agri-Commodity Inflation (2021–2022)**  
   The unprecedented spike in global palm oil, wheat flour, and packaging material prices severely compressed EBITDA margins from ~16% down to ~11–12%. Given the competitive nature of retail biscuits, price hikes could only be passed on with a 1-to-2 quarter lag.

2. **Gestation Lag from Aggressive Green/Brownfield Capex**  
   The company initiated heavy capital expenditure (expanding Rajpura, Greater Noida, and Khordha facilities). Operating leverage temporarily deteriorated as fixed depreciation and overhead costs scaled up ahead of revenue realization.

3. **Post-COVID Normalization in In-Home Biscuits Consumption**  
   The extraordinary surge in packaged food demand during pandemic lockdowns naturally normalized in FY22–FY23, leading to temporary volume growth deceleration in the retail biscuits vertical.

4. **Institutional Supply Overhang Post-IPO Lockup**  
   Early PE investors (GW European Investment Holding, Sequoia) offloaded equity stakes upon lockup expiry, creating secondary market supply pressure that capped upside momentum despite solid baseline execution.

### 2. Why Did the Stock Fall Earlier?

Prior cyclical pullbacks and multi-year underperformance periods (e.g., 2018–2020 and interim margin compressions in 2022) were driven by five distinct structural root causes:

1. **Capital Allocation Drag & Sub-Scale Global Investments (2018–2020):**
   M&M historically destroyed return ratios through persistent capital deployment into loss-making global subsidiaries, including SsangYong Motor (Korea), GenZe, Mahindra North America Technical Center (MANA), and MANA aircraft/automotive ventures. Return on Equity (ROE) dropped into single digits (~8–10%).

2. **Mid-SUV Market Share Loss to Korean & Domestic Peers (2017–2021):**
   Before the launch of the Thar, XUV700, and Scorpio-N, M&M lost significant market share in the soft-roader / monocoque SUV segment to Hyundai (Creta), Kia (Seltos), and Tata Motors. Its legacy portfolio (KUV100, TUV300, Marazzo) failed to generate traction.

3. **Farm Equipment Sector Cyclical Slowdown & Monsoon Vulnerability:**
   Tractor volume growth slowed down sharply due to erratic spatial monsoon distribution, rising rural input costs, and delayed harvest cycles, impacting the high-margin Farm Equipment Sector (FES).

4. **Severe Chip Shortages & Supply Chain Bottlenecks (2021–2022):**
   Despite accumulating over 200,000 open bookings for the XUV700 and Thar, production bottlenecks due to global semiconductor shortages constrained delivery capability, inflating order cancellation risks and deferring revenue realization.

5. **Raw Material Cost Inflation & Operating Margin Compression (2022):**
   Hyper-inflation in steel, precious metals (rhodium, palladium for catalysts), and aluminum compressed automotive EBITDA margins from historical ~13-14% levels down to ~11.5%.
