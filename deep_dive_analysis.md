### 2. Why Did the Stock Fall Earlier?

Persistent Systems experienced temporary valuation compressions and sharp cyclical pullbacks over the past 12–18 months. Ranked root causes behind prior corrections:

1. **Global Tech Discretionary Spend Freeze**: Heightened macroeconomic uncertainty and elevated interest rates across North America and Europe caused enterprise clients to pause non-critical discretionary IT spending, particularly in legacy transformation projects.
2. **BFSI & Telecom Vertical Headwinds**: Prolonged softness in North American BFSI regional banking client budgets and global telecom spending led to extended deal cycles and delayed revenue conversion from TCV (Total Contract Value).
3. **Margin Compression via Onsite Cost Inflation**: A temporary spike in onsite subcontracting costs and wage inflation across critical tech skill sets (Cloud Architecture, AI/ML) compressed operating margins (EBIT) toward the 14.0% threshold.
4. **Peer Valuation Multiple Normalization**: High baseline trailing multiples (P/E > 50x) rendered the stock vulnerable to profit-taking during sector-wide risk-off rotations in midcap IT services.
